Limits you should know before you apply these in Google Slides:

- **Pie chart:** The builder draws clustered column charts only in Slides. Slide 2 uses a column chart for Enterprise 55, Mid-market 30, Small business 15 instead of a pie.
- **Speaker notes:** `notes` in the outline apply to PowerPoint only. They are not in the Slides fill batches. Add notes in Slides after import, or use PowerPoint if you need notes in the file.
- **Team photo:** Slides ignores `image` on `photo-right`. Slide 3 has the title "Meet the team"; insert `examples/images/sample-photo.png` manually (Insert > Image) on that layout.
- **Orange titles:** Title color comes from the Workday template (Ink). Custom orange title styling is not supported in the outline.
